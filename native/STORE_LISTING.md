## Description (EN)
Landing Pack is the all-in-one survival toolkit for Chinese students heading abroad.
Before you leave and after you land, Landing Pack helps you:

- ✅ Pre-arrival checklist — visa documents, packing, and school-specific tasks for 10 schools across 5 cities.
- 🗺 City survival guides — London, New York, Sydney, Toronto, Tokyo: SIM, banking, housing, food, work.
- 💬 Peer Q&A — ask seniors at your school, in Chinese or English.
- 🛒 Essentials shop — curated partner deals (eSIM, insurance, flights, banking) with no extra cost to you.
- 📦 Landing Kits — bilingual digital packs (visa, lease, all-in-one) you buy once and keep forever.
- 🎓 Mentor booking — 1:1 video consults with senior students.

Bilingual (中文 / English) throughout. Built for students from Shanghai and across China
studying in the UK, US, Australia, Canada, New Zealand, Japan and beyond.
