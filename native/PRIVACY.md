# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策
**留学生落地包 / Landing Pack**

_Last updated: 2026-08-10_

## English
Landing Pack ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and why.

**Information we collect**
- **Account data**: email and a display name you provide to register.
- **Usage data**: checklists you complete, Kits you purchase, bookings you make, and Q&A you post.
- **Payment data**: handled by our payment partners (Stripe / WeChat Pay / Alipay). We do not store your card or bank details.
- **Partner referrals**: when you tap an affiliate link in the Shop, the partner may set a cookie to attribute the referral. We earn a commission; your price is unchanged.

**How we use it**
- To provide the App (save your progress, unlock purchased Kits, show your bookings).
- To improve content (e.g., moderation of Q&A).
- We do **not** sell your personal data.

**Your rights**
- You can delete your account and data by contacting us.
- You can request a copy of your data.

**Children**
- The App is intended for students aged 16+. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children.

**Contact**
- admin@landing.pack

## 中文
留学生落地包（"本应用"）重视您的隐私。本政策说明我们收集哪些信息及用途。

**我们收集的信息**
- **账户信息**：您注册时提供的邮箱与昵称。
- **使用数据**：您完成的清单、购买的落地包、预约记录及发布的问答。
- **支付信息**：由支付合作方（Stripe / 微信支付 / 支付宝）处理，我们不存储您的银行卡或银行信息。
- **合作推荐**：当您点击商城中的返佣链接时，合作方可能设置 Cookie 以归因推荐。我们获得佣金，您的价格不变。

**我们如何使用**
- 提供应用功能（保存进度、解锁已购落地包、显示预约）。
- 改进内容（例如问答审核）。
- 我们**不**出售您的个人数据。

**您的权利**
- 可联系我们删除账户与数据。
- 可索取您的数据副本。

**未成年人**
- 本应用面向 16 岁及以上学生，不故意收集低龄儿童数据。

**联系**
- admin@landing.pack
