# Pre-departure Packing & Docs (English)

> For Chinese students heading to the UK / US / AU / CA / JP. Tick your school's checklist in the app first, then use this list.

## 1. Carry ON person (never check in)
- [ ] Passport (valid ≥ 6 months) + visa / BRP / Residence Card
- [ ] Offer letter / CAS / I-20 / CoE / LOA (paper + digital)
- [ ] Flight itinerary, accommodation confirmation
- [ ] Passport photos (white background, several)
- [ ] Medical / vaccination / TB test reports
- [ ] Proof of funds, scholarship / GIC evidence
- [ ] Credit card (enabled for overseas use)
- [ ] Some local cash (taxi / food on arrival)

## 2. Documents & copies (store separately)
- [ ] Passport photo + visa page copies ×2
- [ ] National ID copy (for use back home)
- [ ] School contacts, pickup person's phone (written on paper)

## 3. Daily items
- [ ] Travel adapter + power strip (UK/US/AU/JP plug per country)
- [ ] Common meds (fever, stomach, allergy — check banned ingredients)
- [ ] Glasses / contacts + prescription
- [ ] Seasonal clothing (match your city; winter coats up north)
- [ ] Phone + charger (enable roaming before departure)

## 4. Within 7 days of arrival
- [ ] Collect BRP / Residence Card / student ID
- [ ] Open a bank account (passport + visa + proof of address)
- [ ] Get a local SIM / student discount
- [ ] On-campus enrolment, course registration, transit card
- [ ] Let family know you arrived safely

> More: in the app, pick your school to see "Arrival tips" and your school's checklist.
